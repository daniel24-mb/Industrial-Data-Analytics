# Bright Coffee Shop – Lovable Dashboard

**Live link:**https://bright-coffee-dt-mbenga.lovable.app/

Interactive web dashboard (Jan–Jun 2023), built in Lovable from processed Databricks data.

- KPIs: $698,812 revenue | 149,116 sales | 214,470 units | $4.69 average sale
- Store filter, revenue by store, store share, store revenue by month
- Monthly revenue, day of week, day × time heat map, 30-minute time slots
- Category, top 10, bottom 10 and product type revenue
- Key insights

Prepared by DT Mbenga.
